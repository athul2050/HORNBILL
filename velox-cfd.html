Velox CFD

High-fidelity computational fluid dynamics for industry-scale external aerodynamic flows — engineered indigenously, from solver to workflow.

Explore Platform Capabilities Request Technical Consultation
The Problem

Complex, industry-scale geometry makes external aerodynamic analysis slow and difficult to trust.

Aerospace and defence programs increasingly need aerodynamic answers over geometry too complex, or at a scale too large, for conventional CFD tooling to resolve quickly and reliably.

The Platform

Built for scale, deployed on distributed infrastructure.

Velox CFD combines advanced numerical capabilities with an integrated high-performance computing framework. The software can be deployed on distributed computing facilities, enabling large-scale simulations with competitive turnaround times, while an automated simulation workflow minimizes manual intervention without compromising accuracy or reliability.
