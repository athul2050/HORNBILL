Rocketry Workshop

Hands-on exposure to the development, assembly, and launching of a model rocket — for young and enthusiastic students.

Enquire about this Program
What It Is

From fundamentals to a real launch, in one day.

With the rapid growth of the space, aerospace, and defence sectors, the design and development of launch vehicles and rocket systems have gained significant importance in recent years. This workshop introduces participants to the fundamentals of model rocketry, key structural and system-level components, assembly procedures, and basic principles of rocket flight — concluding with a practical launching session.

What You'll Do

An application-oriented walk through the full build.

Who It's For

Young, enthusiastic students.

Designed for participants curious about launch vehicles and rocket systems, with no prior experience required — an engaging, application-oriented introduction to the complete model rocket development process.
